# Health Insurance Coverage Analysis  
*By Toluwani Emmanuel*  

**Tool Used:** Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning, PivotTables, Charts, Slicers)  

## Objective  
The goal of this project was to analyze the impact of the Affordable Care Act (ACA) on health insurance coverage in the United States. The dashboard highlights uninsured rate trends, Medicaid expansion, marketplace enrollment, and state-level performance to support policy evaluation and strategic planning.  

## Data Preparation  
- Dataset compiled from the **US Department of Health and Human Services** and the **US Census Bureau**.  
- Cleaned data by removing duplicates and blanks.  
- Standardized column headers and verified data types.  
- Built PivotTables to power all visuals.  
- Added interactive slicers for **State**, **Medicaid Expansion Status (2016)**, and **Uninsured Rate Change (2010–2015)**.  

## KPIs Displayed  
- Average Uninsured Rate (2010 vs 2015)  
- Top 5 States with Greatest Decline in Uninsured Rate  
- Medicaid Expansion & Marketplace Enrollment (2016)  
- Nationwide Uninsured Rate (Actual vs Projection, 2010–2030)  
- Top 10 States by Medicaid Enrollment Growth (2013–2016)  
- Uninsured Rate Change by Expansion Status (2010–2015)  

## Key Insights  

1. **Average Uninsured Rate Before and After ACA**  
   - 2010: 14.18% uninsured.  
   - 2015: 8.73% uninsured.  
   - Decline of 5.45 percentage points shows ACA’s strong early impact.  

2. **Top 5 States with Greatest Decline (2010–2015)**  
   - Connecticut recorded the largest drop (–3.10%).  
   - Massachusetts had the smallest decline among the top five (–1.60%).  
   - Highlights states that benefited most in reducing uninsured populations.  

3. **Medicaid Expansion & Marketplace Enrollment (2016)**  
   - Expansion states: Massachusetts highest (4.23M), North Dakota lowest (450K).  
   - Non-expansion states: Maine highest (702K), South Dakota lowest (449K).  
   - Shows how expansion decisions influenced marketplace participation.  

4. **Nationwide Uninsured Rate — Actual vs Projection (2010–2030)**  
   - 2010: 14.18% → 2015: 8.73%.  
   - Projected 2025: ~8.0%, 2030: ~7.0%.  
   - Suggests early ACA impact was strongest, with slower gains expected over time.  

5. **Top 10 States by Medicaid Enrollment Growth (2013–2016)**  
   - US overall: +16M new enrollees.  
   - New Jersey smallest growth (~500K).  
   - Demonstrates Medicaid expansion’s role in reducing uninsured rates nationwide.  

6. **Uninsured Rate Change by Expansion Status (2010–2015)**  
   - Expansion states: –5.86% average decline.  
   - Non-expansion states: –4.72% average decline.  
   - Confirms Medicaid expansion as a key driver in reducing uninsured populations.  

## Outcome  
This dashboard demonstrates how the Affordable Care Act significantly reduced the uninsured rate nationwide, with expansion states benefiting the most. It highlights the role of Medicaid enrollment and marketplace participation in sustaining coverage gains, while projecting future trends in insurance coverage.
