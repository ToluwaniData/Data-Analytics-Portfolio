# Fashion and Beauty Supply Chain Analysis  
*By Toluwani Emmanuel*  

**Tool Used:** Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning, PivotTables, Charts, Slicers)  

## Objective  
The goal of this project was to analyze the supply chain performance of a fashion and beauty retailer. The dashboard highlights product sales, shipping costs, defect rates, and inventory levels to support strategic decisions in operations and customer satisfaction.  

## Data Preparation  
- Removed duplicates and blanks.  
- Standardized column headers.  
- Verified data types and ensured consistency across product categories and transport modes.  
- Structured the dataset into an Excel table and powered visuals with PivotTables.  
- Added interactive slicers for **Product Type**, **Shipping Carrier**, and **Transport Mode**.  

## KPIs Displayed  
- **Total Revenue Generated**  
- **Total Products Sold**  
- **Top-Selling Product Type**  
- **Total Records in Dataset**  
- **Average Defect Rate**  
- **Highest Revenue Product Type**  

## Key Insights  

1. **Price vs Revenue by Product Type (Clustered Column Chart):**  
   - Skincare generated the highest revenue ($241,628).  
   - Cosmetics had the lowest revenue ($161,521) but the highest average price ($57.36).  
   - Haircare had the lowest average price ($46.01).  
   - Insight: Higher price doesn’t always mean higher revenue — sales volume matters.  

2. **Sales Distribution by Product Type (Donut Chart):**  
   - Skincare leads with 45% of total sales.  
   - Cosmetics trails with 25.5%.  
   - Reinforces Skincare as both top-selling and top-revenue product.  

3. **Revenue by Shipping Carrier (Column Chart):**  
   - Carrier B leads with $250,094.  
   - Carrier A lags with $142,630.  
   - Suggests differences in delivery speed, reliability, or coverage.  

4. **Top 10 SKUs by Revenue (Line Chart):**  
   - SKU51 peaked at $9,866.47.  
   - SKU18 lowest among top 10 at $9,364.67.  
   - Narrow revenue gap shows consistent performance across top SKUs.  

5. **Top 10 Stock Levels by SKU (Line Chart):**  
   - SKU12, SKU51, and SKU59 had highest stock (100 units).  
   - SKU45 and SKU93 lowest (93 units).  
   - Helps monitor inventory balance.  

6. **Shipping Cost by Carrier (Column Chart):**  
   - Carrier B most expensive ($236.89).  
   - Carrier A cheapest ($155.54).  
   - Impacts profitability and customer delivery charges.  

7. **Cost Distribution by Transport Mode (Column Chart):**  
   - Road most expensive ($160.73).  
   - Sea cheapest ($84.49).  
   - Insight: Transport choice directly affects shipping costs.  

8. **Average Defect Rate by Product Type & Transport Mode (Clustered Column Chart):**  
   - Haircare shows highest defect rates (Road 4.00%, Sea 3.50%).  
   - Skincare moderate (~3.00% via Rail/Road).  
   - Cosmetics via Air lowest defect rate (0.50%).  
   - Insight: Haircare via Road may need better packaging; Cosmetics via Air is most reliable.  

## Outcome  
This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of supply chain performance across products, carriers, and transport modes. It highlights cost efficiency, product quality, and inventory management, supporting strategic decisions in operations and customer engagement.
