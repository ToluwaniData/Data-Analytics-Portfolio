# Supply Chain Greenhouse Gas Emissions Analysis  
*By Toluwani Emmanuel*  

## Objective  
This project analyzes greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions across different sectors of the economy, measured in CO₂-equivalent emissions per dollar spent. The dashboard highlights both direct emissions and margin emissions (supply chain emissions), providing a full picture of environmental impact.  

## Tools Used  
- **Power BI** (dashboard design and visualization)  
- **Power Query** (data cleaning and transformation)  
- **DAX** (custom measures and calculations)  

## Data Preparation  
- Dataset compiled from the **Supply Chain Greenhouse Gas Emissions dataset**.  
- Built a unified data model with measures for direct, margin, and total emissions.  
- Added interactive slicers for:  
  - **Margin Share Type** (High, Medium, Low)  
  - **Emission Gap** (numeric range)  
  - **Margin Share** (percentage slider)  

## KPIs Displayed  
- Direct Emissions  
- Margin Emissions  
- Total Emissions  
- Margin Share  
- Emission Gap  

## Key Insights  

1. **Top 10 Sectors by Margin Emissions (Column Chart)**  
   - Anthracite Mining has the highest margin factor (0.27).  
   - Potash, Soda & Borate Mineral Mining has the lowest (0.21).  
   - Insight: Shows which sectors pollute more through supply chains than direct operations.  

2. **Top 5 Sectors by Total Emissions (Column Chart)**  
   - Solid Waste Landfill leads with an emission factor of 10.99.  
   - Dual-Purpose Cattle Ranching & Farming is lowest at 3.30.  
   - Insight: Identifies sectors producing the most emissions per dollar spent.  

3. **Margin Share by Sector (Column Chart)**  
   - Other Measuring & Controlling Device Manufacturing has the highest margin share (67.0%).  
   - Sound Recording Studios lowest at 63.2%.  
   - Insight: Even sectors with lower total emissions can have high indirect supply chain emissions.  

4. **Direct vs Total Emissions (Stacked Bar Chart)**  
   - Solid Waste Landfill shows the largest difference between direct and total emissions (10.99 vs slightly lower direct).  
   - Dual-Purpose Cattle Ranching & Farming shows minimal difference (3.30 vs 3.23).  
   - Insight: Highlights how supply chain emissions widen the gap in total emissions.  

5. **Top 3 Sectors by Margin Emissions (Donut Chart)**  
   - Anthracite Mining, Bituminous Coal & Lignite Surface Mining, and Bituminous Coal Underground Mining each contribute 33.33%.  
   - Insight: These sectors are critical targets for sustainability efforts.  

## Outcome  
This dashboard provides a clear view of how greenhouse gas emissions vary across sectors — not just direct emissions, but also hidden supply chain impacts. It highlights industries with the highest total emissions, those most reliant on margin emissions, and where sustainability efforts can make the biggest difference.
