# Retail Sales and Customer Demographics Analysis  
*By Toluwani Emmanuel*  

## Objective  
This project analyzes retail sales and customer demographics using Power BI. The goal was to uncover patterns in customer spending, product preferences, and seasonal trends, providing insights that can guide marketing strategies, inventory planning, and customer engagement initiatives.  

## Tools Used  
- **Power BI** (dashboard design and visualization)  
- **Power Query** (data cleaning and transformation)  
- **DAX** (custom measures and calculations)  

## Data Preparation  
- Cleaned and modeled retail sales dataset.  
- Added interactive slicers for **Product Category**, **Age Group**, **Month**, and **Year**.  
- Card visuals display the 5 core KPIs at the top of the dashboard for quick insights.  

## KPIs Displayed  
- Average Revenue by Age Group & Gender  
- Total Revenue by Product Category  
- Average Purchase Volume per Transaction  
- Average Price per Unit by Product Category  
- Monthly Sales Trends  

## Key Insights  

1. **Average Revenue by Age Group & Gender**  
   - Young adult females: highest average revenue (₦502.23).  
   - Adult males: highest among men (₦479.22).  
   - Senior females: lowest overall (₦319.76).  
   - Insight: Younger females and adult males are the most valuable segments in terms of spending.  

2. **Revenue by Product Category**  
   - Electronics: highest total revenue (₦156,905).  
   - Beauty products: lowest (₦143,515).  
   - Insight: Electronics are the strongest driver of sales.  

3. **Purchase Volume by Age Group**  
   - Older adult females: highest average (2.60 units per transaction).  
   - Young adult males: highest among men (2.53 units).  
   - Senior females: lowest (2.33 units).  
   - Insight: Older females and younger males tend to buy more items per purchase.  

4. **Average Price per Unit by Product Category (Bar Chart)**  
   - Beauty products: highest average price (₦184.06).  
   - Clothing: lowest (₦174.29).  
   - Insight: Beauty items are priced at a premium compared to clothing.  

5. **Monthly Sales Trends**  
   - Peak: May 2023 (₦53,150).  
   - Lowest: January 2024 (₦1,530).  
   - Insight: Clear seasonal fluctuations in customer spending.  

6. **Revenue Breakdown by Age Group & Product Category**  
   - Electronics: older adults contributed the highest (₦51,920).  
   - Clothing: young adults led (₦48,670).  
   - Beauty: older adults topped (₦47,540), but young adults and adults were close behind.  
   - Insight: Product preferences shift across age groups.  

7. **Seasonal Revenue Trends**  
   - Beauty peaked in October 2023 (₦15,355).  
   - Clothing peaked in May 2023 (₦17,455).  
   - Electronics peaked at ₦23,245.  
   - Lowest point: January 2024 (Beauty ₦1,500, Electronics ₦30, Clothing no record).  
   - Insight: January 2024 was the weakest month overall, while mid-to-late 2023 showed strong seasonal peaks.  

## Outcome  
This dashboard provides a clear picture of retail performance and customer demographics. It highlights which age groups and genders drive the most revenue, which product categories dominate sales, and how seasonal trends impact customer behavior.
