# Global Mental Health Disorder Analysis  
*By Toluwani Emmanuel*  

## Objective  
This project analyzes global mental health disorder trends from 1990 to 2020 using Power BI. The dashboard explores seven major disorders: **depression, anxiety, alcohol use disorder, drug use disorder, bipolar disorder, eating disorder, and schizophrenia**. The goal was to understand how prevalence varies across countries and over time, highlighting global and regional differences.  

## Tools Used  
- **Power BI** (dashboard design and visualization)  
- **Power Query** (data cleaning and transformation)  
- **DAX** (custom measures and calculations)  

## Data Preparation  
- Dataset compiled from global mental health prevalence statistics (1990–2020).  
- Built a unified data model with measures for each disorder.  
- Added interactive slicers for **Year** and **Country**.  
- Card visuals display the average prevalence of each disorder to provide a big-picture overview.  

## KPIs Displayed  
- Average prevalence of depression, anxiety, alcohol use disorder, drug use disorder, bipolar disorder, eating disorder, and schizophrenia.  

## Key Insights  

1. **Top 5 Countries by Depression Prevalence (%)**  
   - Greenland: highest average prevalence (6.46%).  
   - Finland: lowest average prevalence (5.18%).  
   - Insight: Even within the same region, countries can experience very different levels of mental health challenges.  

2. **Anxiety Disorder Prevalence Across Countries (%)**  
   - New Zealand: highest average (8.65%).  
   - Australia: lowest average (6.90%).  
   - Insight: Neighboring countries can have different experiences with anxiety, influenced by social or cultural factors.  

3. **Global Trend of Depression Prevalence (1990–2020)**  
   - Peak: 1999 (3.53%).  
   - Lowest averages: 2011–2017 (3.46%).  
   - Insight: Depression prevalence has fluctuated globally, with certain periods showing higher impact.  

4. **Bipolar Disorder Cases in Nigeria, Ghana, and Kenya**  
   - Nigeria: highest average cases (1,339).  
   - Kenya: 1,185 cases.  
   - Ghana: lowest (1,111).  
   - Insight: Regional comparison highlights differences in bipolar disorder prevalence across African countries.  

5. **Top 5 Countries: Alcohol vs Drug Use Disorders**  
   - Drug use disorder: Estonia highest (1.02), Ukraine lowest (0.65).  
   - Alcohol use disorder: Belarus highest (5.29), Estonia lowest (4.92).  
   - Insight: Substance-related disorders vary widely across countries, showing strong regional differences.  

6. **Global Trend of Anxiety Disorder Prevalence (1990–2020)**  
   - Rising trend from 2002–2017 (around 4.00%).  
   - Lowest in 1990 (3.96%).  
   - Insight: Anxiety has become more widespread globally over the years.  

## Outcome  
This dashboard highlights how mental health disorders differ across countries and over time. Depression and anxiety remain major global challenges, while substance-related disorders show strong regional differences. By visualizing this data, stakeholders can better understand the scale of these issues and the need for continued awareness and intervention.
