# Amazon E-Commerce Sales Analysis  
*By Toluwani Emmanuel*  

## Objective  
This project analyzes Amazon e-commerce sales data using Power BI. The goal was to explore product performance — focusing on reviews, ratings, discounts, and categories — and uncover insights about customer behavior and pricing strategies.  

## Tools Used  
- **Power BI** (dashboard design and visualization)  
- **Power Query** (data cleaning and transformation)  
- **DAX** (custom measures and calculations)  

## Data Preparation  
- Cleaned and modeled Amazon e-commerce dataset.  
- Added interactive slicers for **Category** and **Discount Percentage**.  
- Card visuals display five key metrics at the top of the dashboard.  

## KPIs Displayed  
- Total Products: 1,350  
- Total Reviews: 1,464  
- Average Rating: 4.1  
- Total Product Categories: 9  
- Average Discount Percentage: 48%  

## Key Insights  

1. **Top 6 Products by Average Rating (Bar Chart)**  
   - Amazon Basics Wireless Mouse: highest rating (5.0).  
   - Swiffer Instant Electric Water Heater: lowest among top group (4.8).  
   - Insight: Certain products delight customers more, setting benchmarks for satisfaction.  

2. **Price vs Rating Analysis (Line + Clustered Column Chart)**  
   - Highly Rated products: highest average actual price (6,628.95 INR).  
   - Average Rated products: lowest average actual price (1,576.89 INR).  
   - Discounted prices: Highly Rated (3,884.06 INR) vs Average Rated (458.44 INR).  
   - Insight: Customers are willing to pay more for higher-rated products, even after discounts.  

3. **Category Performance: Ratings vs Reviews**  
   - Electronics: highest reviews (526).  
   - Toys & Games: lowest reviews (1).  
   - Ratings: Office Products, Home Improvement, Toys & Games (4.3 average).  
   - Car & Motorbike: lowest rating (3.8).  
   - Insight: Some categories attract more reviews, while others maintain quality despite fewer customers.  

4. **Top 6 Most Reviewed Categories**  
   - Electronics: most engaged (526 reviews).  
   - Musical Instruments: lowest (2 reviews).  
   - Insight: Electronics dominate customer engagement, while niche categories show limited feedback.  

5. **Top 5 Categories by Average Discount Percentage**  
   - Home Improvement: highest average discount (0.58).  
   - Musical Instruments: lowest average discount (0.46).  
   - Insight: Discount strategies vary widely across categories.  

6. **Top 5 Products by Discount Percentage**  
   - Product ID B097C564GC: highest discount (0.94).  
   - Four other products: discounts of 0.91 each.  
   - Insight: Certain products are heavily discounted, likely to drive sales or clear inventory.  

## Outcome  
This dashboard provides a clear view of how ratings, reviews, prices, and discounts interact across categories and products. It highlights customer satisfaction trends, pricing strategies, and discount practices, offering valuable insights for e-commerce optimization.
