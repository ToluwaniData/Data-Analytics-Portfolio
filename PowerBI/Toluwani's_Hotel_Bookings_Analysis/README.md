# Hotel Bookings Analysis  
*By Toluwani Emmanuel*  

## Objective  
This project analyzes hotel booking trends using Power BI. The dashboard highlights traveler demographics, pricing differences, booking channels, customer types, and seasonal patterns to provide a comprehensive view of hotel performance.  

## Tools Used  
- **Power BI** (dashboard design and visualization)  
- **Power Query** (data cleaning and transformation)  
- **DAX** (custom measures and calculations)  

## Data Preparation  
- Cleaned dataset and replaced blanks with “Unknown” for clarity.  
- Built a unified data model for bookings by country, hotel type, market segment, and distribution channel.  
- Added interactive slicers for **Month**, **Hotel Type**, and **Market Segment**.  
- Card visuals display key metrics at the top of the dashboard.  

## KPIs Displayed  
- Total Bookings  
- Average Daily Rate (ADR)  
- Bookings by Market Segment  
- Bookings by Distribution Channel  
- Children and Babies by Customer Type  
- Monthly Bookings Trend  

## Key Insights  

1. **Top 5 Countries by Travelers**  
   - Portugal (PRT): highest with ~46,590 bookings.  
   - Germany (DEU): lowest among top five with ~7,287 bookings.  
   - Insight: Highlights major source markets and smaller contributors.  

2. **Highest ADR by Hotel Type**  
   - City Hotel: highest ADR ($105.30).  
   - Resort Hotel: lowest ADR ($94.95).  
   - Insight: Pricing differs depending on hotel type.  

3. **Bookings by Market Segment**  
   - Online Travel Agents: highest (~56,477 bookings).  
   - Aviation: lowest.  
   - Insight: Reveals most popular booking channels and least significant ones.  

4. **Children and Babies by Customer Type**  
   - Transient customers: highest (~12,110).  
   - Group customers: lowest (~43).  
   - Insight: Transient customers are more likely to travel with families.  

5. **Bookings by Distribution Channel**  
   - Travel Agent/Tour Operator: highest (~97,870 bookings).  
   - GDS: lowest (~193 bookings).  
   - Insight: Identifies which distribution channels drive the most bookings.  

6. **Monthly Bookings Trend**  
   - August: peak month (~13,877 bookings).  
   - January: lowest (~5,929 bookings).  
   - Insight: Shows seasonal booking patterns with peak and off‑peak periods.  

## Outcome  
This dashboard provides clear insights into traveler demographics, hotel pricing, booking channels, and seasonal trends. It helps stakeholders understand where most travelers come from, how much they pay, how they book, and when bookings are highest or lowest.
