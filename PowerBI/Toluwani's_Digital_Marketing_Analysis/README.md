# Digital Marketing Analysis  
*By Toluwani Emmanuel*  

## Objective  
This project analyzes digital marketing campaign performance using Power BI. The dataset contains daily records of marketing spend, impressions, clicks, leads, orders, and revenue across different campaign types and platforms. The goal was to evaluate campaign efficiency, profitability, and customer behavior.  

## Tools Used  
- **Power BI** (dashboard design and visualization)  
- **Power Query** (data cleaning and transformation)  
- **DAX** (custom measures and calculations)  

## Data Preparation  
- Cleaned and modeled digital marketing dataset.  
- Added interactive slicers for **Date**, **Category**, and **Campaign Name**.  
- Card visuals display key KPIs for quick insights.  

## KPIs Displayed  
- Return on Marketing Investment (ROMI)  
- Average Order Value (AOV)  
- Gross Profit  
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)  
- Conversion Rates  
- Cost per Click (CPC)  
- Cost per Lead (CPL)  
- Click‑Through Rate (CTR)  

## Key Insights  

1. **Top 5 Campaigns by ROMI (Bar Chart)**  
   - YouTube Blogger: highest ROMI (277.3%).  
   - Instagram Blogger: lowest ROMI (36.8%).  
   - Insight: Identifies most profitable campaigns versus those draining resources.  

2. **Buyers Activity by Day of Week (Bar Chart)**  
   - Friday: peak day (1,674 orders).  
   - Sunday: lowest activity (651 orders).  
   - Insight: Buyers are more engaged towards the end of the work week.  

3. **Average Revenue (Weekday vs Weekend)**  
   - Weekdays: highest average revenue ($141,914).  
   - Weekends: lower average revenue ($132,594).  
   - Insight: Weekday orders tend to be of higher value.  

4. **Revenue by Category (Bar Chart)**  
   - Influencer campaigns: highest revenue ($21.1M+).  
   - Search campaigns: lowest revenue ($3.7M).  
   - Insight: Influencer marketing is the most effective driver of revenue.  

5. **Daily Advertising Spend vs Revenue (Line Chart)**  
   - February 20th: highest spend ($3.49M) and highest revenue ($5.26M).  
   - February 28th: lowest spend ($53,363) and lowest revenue ($76,164).  
   - Insight: Identifies days with best returns and underperformance.  

6. **Daily Conversion Rates (Line Chart)**  
   - Conversion Rate 1 (leads-to-clicks): peaked at 2.8% (Feb 10), lowest at 1.3% (Feb 2).  
   - Conversion Rate 2 (orders-to-leads): peaked at 15% (Feb 25), lowest at 9% (Feb 1).  
   - Insight: Shows funnel efficiency from clicks to leads and leads to sales.  

## Outcome  
This dashboard provides a clear picture of digital marketing performance. It highlights the most profitable campaigns, buyer activity patterns, revenue drivers, and conversion funnel strengths and weaknesses. These insights help guide investment decisions, targeting strategies, and campaign optimization.
