# Car Insurance Analysis  
*By Toluwani Emmanuel*  

## Objective  
This project analyzes car insurance data to uncover patterns in claim frequency, claim amounts, and customer demographics. The dashboard provides insights into how demographics, car attributes, and financial factors influence insurance claims, helping insurers spot risk patterns, adjust coverage policies, and refine pricing strategies.  

## Tools Used  
- **Power BI** (dashboard design and visualization)  
- **Power Query** (data cleaning and transformation)  
- **DAX** (custom measures and calculations)  

## Data Preparation  
- Cleaned and modeled car insurance dataset.  
- Added interactive slicers for **Gender**, **Household Income Group**, **Coverage Zone**, and **Age Group**.  
- Card visuals display key metrics: **Total Car Use**, **Total Household Income**, **Total Claims Paid**, **Total Coverage Zones**, and **Total Parents**.  

## KPIs Displayed  
- Total Car Use  
- Total Household Income  
- Total Claims Paid  
- Total Coverage Zones  
- Total Parents  

## Key Insights  

1. **Distribution of Car Usage by Gender**  
   - Private car use among females: 15,071 (highest).  
   - Private car use among males: 14,989.  
   - Commercial use nearly equal: males (3,747), females (3,735).  
   - Insight: Car usage patterns are balanced across genders, with private use dominating overall.  

2. **Claim Frequency by Education Level**  
   - Bachelor’s degree holders: highest claim frequency (9,500).  
   - PhD holders: lowest (1,382).  
   - Insight: Claim activity is concentrated among customers with bachelor’s education.  

3. **Average Claim Amount by Education Level**  
   - Bachelor’s degree holders: highest ($50,276).  
   - PhD holders: lowest ($49,556).  
   - Insight: Education level influences both claim frequency and claim size.  

4. **Average Claim Amount by Coverage Zone**  
   - Urban areas: highest ($50,378).  
   - Rural areas: lowest ($49,778).  
   - Insight: Urban claims tend to be more costly, possibly due to higher accident rates or repair costs.  

5. **Claim Frequency and Average Claim Amount by Car Make & Model**  
   - Ford: highest claim frequency (1,694).  
   - Fairthorpe, Hilman, Shelby: no claims.  
   - Hilman: highest average claim amount ($79,049).  
   - Rambler: lowest ($16,604).  
   - Insight: Some brands are more frequently involved in claims, while others have fewer but costlier claims.  

6. **Car Color vs Claim Frequency and Claim Amount**  
   - Aquamarine: highest claim frequency (1,094).  
   - Turquoise: lowest (1,041).  
   - Green cars: highest average claim amount ($50,821).  
   - Aquamarine cars: lowest ($49,809).  
   - Insight: Car color may subtly influence both claim frequency and cost.  

7. **Household Income vs Claim Frequency and Claim Amount**  
   - Very high income households: highest claim frequency (13,039).  
   - Middle income households: lowest (1,421).  
   - High income households: highest average claim amount ($50,111).  
   - Middle income households: lowest ($49,763).  
   - Insight: Higher income groups file more claims and tend to have slightly higher claim amounts.  

## Outcome  
This dashboard provides a clear picture of how demographics, car attributes, and financial factors influence insurance claims. It helps insurers identify risk patterns, adjust coverage policies, and refine pricing strategies.
